Proposal tracker

Fees requested from and quoted by each discipline
Link accepted proposals to discipline fees
Master switch. Off, this screen is a log — you can track, compare and accept without anything moving. On, every accepted line writes its approved fee into the mapped discipline, that row on the Disciplines screen turns read-only with a link marker, and your hand-entered figures are held so flipping back restores them exactly. Accepting is per line; whether accepting changes the budget is this one switch.

Proposals

Range shows where the submitted fee falls between expected low and high Rejected lines keep their numbers for comparison Drag any column edge to resize · double-click to reset
One line per proposal, not per discipline. Two firms bidding civil get two lines mapped to the same discipline, side by side with their ranges. Accepted fees for a discipline are summed, and a warning appears above if more than one is live at once. Architecture is the residual and cannot be mapped — a proposal against it stays unassigned.

Scope packages

Only if the contract invoices in parts
Most jobs never need this. A scope package is a slice of the same contract that bills on its own — two campuses on one agreement, or a phase funded from a different pot. Give the package a share and hit Fill from share to spread it across the disciplines proportionally, or type each discipline directly. The footer tracks allocated against contract so packages always reconcile, and empty packages are dropped from every export.
